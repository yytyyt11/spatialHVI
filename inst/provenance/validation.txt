spatialHVI 0.1.0 validation, 17 November 2026

Environment: R 4.4.1 on Windows, single-thread BLAS/OpenMP.

Package tests: 73 passed, 0 failures, 0 warnings, 0 skips.
Source-package installation and installed synthetic example: passed.
R CMD check --no-manual --ignore-vignettes: Status OK.
The PDF reference manual and vignette rebuilding were not part of that check.

Frozen implementation comparison used two small synthetic data sets with
generating rho = 0.20 and 0.97, six traits and forty subjects. At both the
manuscript ELBO tolerance (1e-4) and a tighter numerical tolerance (1e-7),
all checked original MFVI state fields were identical. At the tighter
tolerance, LRVB covariance matrices and interval endpoints were also identical.
The maximum scaled Jacobian errors were 3.99e-11 and 3.28e-8; maximum
perturbation-refit errors were 1.78e-7 and 1.26e-7, below the 5e-4 threshold.

The numerical response checks retain the final increment-based perturbation
validator. This solves nonlinear perturbed fixed-point equations and avoids
loss of tiny categorical responses through subtraction of common constants.
It does not replace or modify the LRVB covariance formula.

The PCH correction was checked against generalized eigenvalues and consistent
pair permutations. Joint variational quantiles and bilateral covariance were
checked independently. The four S17 calibration coefficients match the
manuscript at their full reported precision.

These are software and numerical-equivalence checks using synthetic inputs.
No new manuscript experiment or ADNI data analysis was run for this release.
No subject-level ADNI data, GRM, fitted objects or posterior draws are included.
